Artisan Extraction Process

Our artisan extraction process follows traditional wood-pressing methods where carefully selected groundnuts are slowly pressed without excessive heat. This natural technique helps preserve the oil’s original aroma, nutrients, antioxidants, and rich flavor, ensuring pure and healthy groundnut oil in every drop.
